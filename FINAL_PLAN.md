**Follow this file.** It replaces `DECEMBER_PLAN.md`.
**How to use it:** do one step at a time. When a step is done, change `☐` to `✅` and write the date. Don't skip ahead.

## Read this first: the honest truth about "guaranteed"

Nobody can guarantee money from views. The algorithm decides how many views a clip gets. So this plan runs **two money paths at the same time**:

| Path | What it is | How likely it pays by Dec 31 |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Clipping campaigns** (Whop) | Companies pay you per 1,000 views on clips you post | Possible but uncertain. Depends on views |
| **B. Your first paying client** | A local business or creator pays you to make clips **from their own videos** | **Your most reliable path to your first $20.** You set the price and you get paid before you work |

**The finish line:** real money (at least $20, about 15 JOD) **arrives in your account**. If Path A is slow, Path B is designed to get you there. If Path B is slow, Step 16 tells you exactly what to change. You keep going around that loop until someone says yes.

### Quick answers to your questions
- **What is Whop?** A website and app (whop.com) where brands, streamers and podcasters post paid **clipping campaigns**. You make clips from their videos, post them on your own TikTok, Instagram or YouTube, and get paid per 1,000 views. You don't need any followers.
- **Does Whop work in Jordan?** Jordan isn't a sanctioned country. Whop says it pays out to 160–200+ countries, but **it doesn't publish a country-by-country list**, and the withdrawal methods differ by country. **You have to check inside the app.** That's Step 1, and you do it *before* doing any work.
- **Audience country:** your accounts will mostly reach people in Jordan and the region, because TikTok and Instagram show your posts to people near you first. **Some campaigns only pay for views from the US, UK, Canada or Australia. Skip those.** Choose campaigns that say "worldwide," "all countries," or that don't restrict countries. Do **not** use a VPN or a foreign SIM to fake your location. Campaigns check, and they reject the clips.
- **Ad labels:** turn on the platform's built-in switch every time you post a *campaign* clip (Step 9 shows where it is). Also add #ad if the brief asks for it. It takes 5 seconds, and it protects your account and your payout. Clips you make for paying clients go on *their* accounts, so they don't need it.
- **Taxes:** keep the tracker from Step 5 from day one, with every payment and date. Once the income is regular, ask a Jordanian accountant what you need to declare. At this stage you only need to keep records.
- **Getting clients:** Phase C walks you through it step by step, including the exact messages to send.

# PHASE A — Set up (Tue Nov 24 → Thu Nov 26, about 1.5 hours a day)

### ☐ Step 1 — Make sure you can receive money (30–45 min) · Date done: ____
1. Download the **Whop** app or open whop.com. Sign up with your email.
2. Find **Balances / Payouts / Withdraw** in your profile and start setting up a payout method. **Look at which methods it offers for Jordan** (bank transfer, wire, crypto, PayPal and so on). Take a screenshot.
3. Check that **your Jordanian bank account can receive USD transfers from abroad.** Call the bank or ask in its app. If it can't, open a **Payoneer** account (free) as a backup way to receive money from abroad.
4. For **local clients**, make sure you have **CliQ** set up in your bank app, so a Jordanian client can pay you instantly in JOD.

**Done when:** you have at least **2 working ways to receive money**: a CliQ alias, plus one international method (a Whop payout method or Payoneer).
⚠ If Whop shows **no** payout method for Jordan, skip Path A completely and put all your time into Path B. Path B pays through CliQ, so it always works.

### ☐ Step 2 — Choose your niche (15 min) · Date done: ____
Make **two** choices:
- **For campaigns (Path A):** choose **one** category from what's on Whop that you actually like watching. Business and money podcasts, self-improvement and gaming streams have the most campaigns. Stick to that one niche for all of December.
- **For clients (Path B):** **Jordanian or Arab businesses and creators** who already post videos, such as gyms, personal trainers, dentists and clinics, real estate agents, restaurants and cafés, coaches, car dealers and podcasters. They have long videos or Lives and no time to cut them.

**Done when:** you've written both choices here → Campaign niche: ________ · Client type: ________

### ☐ Step 3 — Create your 3 posting accounts and warm them up (20 min to create, then 15 min a day for 3 days) · Date done: ____
1. Create **one** new account each on **TikTok, Instagram and YouTube**, all with the same name, for your campaign niche. Example: "Money Moments Daily."
2. Add a profile photo and a bio, for example "Best moments from top business podcasts 🎙️".
3. **Warm-up (3 days):** spend 15 minutes a day on each account watching, liking and following videos in your niche. **Don't post yet.**

**Done when:** all 3 accounts exist and you've finished 3 days of warm-up.
⚠ Only one account per platform. Creating lots of accounts on one phone gets them all banned together.

### ☐ Step 4 — Learn CapCut on your phone (45 min) · Date done: ____
1. Install **CapCut**.
2. Practice on any long video you have:
   - Import it and set the ratio to **9:16**.
   - Trim a 30-second part.
   - Zoom or reposition so the speaker's face fills the frame.
   - Add **auto captions** with a bold font and a yellow highlight.
   - Export at **1080p, 30fps**.
3. Keep this practice clip for yourself. **Don't post it.**

**Done when:** you can make one clip in **under 15 minutes**.

### ☐ Step 5 — Create your tracker (15 min) · Date done: ____
Create a Google Sheet called **"Clipping Tracker"** with two tabs:
- **Clips:** date · campaign · platform · link · hook used · submitted? · views at 24h · approved/rejected · reason · $ earned
- **Money:** date · source (Whop / client name) · amount · method (CliQ / bank / Payoneer) · received?

**Done when:** the sheet exists and you can open it on your phone.

# PHASE B — Clipping campaigns (from Fri Nov 27 · keep posting until Dec 18)

### ☐ Step 6 — Join 3 campaigns (Friday, 1 hour) · Date done: ____
In Whop, open **Content Rewards** (the clipping section) and connect your 3 accounts. Then only join campaigns that pass **every** check below:

- ☐ It accepts views from **any country** (or doesn't restrict countries)
- ☐ It pays **$1 or more per 1,000 views**
- ☐ **More than half of the budget is still left**
- ☐ It has a **low minimum views per clip**
- ☐ Its deadline is **after Dec 18**
- ☐ Its source is long podcasts or streams, so you can make many clips from one video

**Read each brief twice.** Write down its rules (length, required tags, ad label, banned topics) in your tracker.

**Done when:** you've joined 3 campaigns and written down their rules.

### ☐ Step 7 — Post your first 3 clips (Friday or Saturday) · Date done: ____
For each clip:
1. Download the source video **the campaign gives you**.
2. Find a moment that **starts strong**: a surprising claim, a number, or a conflict. No "so…" or "um…" at the start.
3. Cut **20–45 seconds** (or whatever length the brief says).
4. Use one of the hooks from our earlier list:
   - Redacted word: "I lost ████ in one night"
   - Starting mid-sentence on the strongest word
   - The exact number the speaker said
5. Post the clip, then follow **Step 9's checklist**.

**Done when:** 3 clips are posted and submitted on Whop.

### ☐ Step 8 — Follow the daily routine (every day until Dec 18) · Started: ____
| Day | Time | Do |
|---|---|---|
| Mon–Thu | 1–2 h | **2 clips** (post each one on TikTok, Instagram **and** YouTube, with a slightly different hook on each) + 30 min on Phase C |
| Fri–Sun | 4–5 h | **5–6 clips** + 2 hours on Phase C |

That works out to about **25 clips a week** across your 3 platforms.

### ☐ Step 9 — Checklist for every campaign clip (memorize this) · Date learned: ____
- ☐ It follows the brief: length, required tags and mentions, no banned topics
- ☐ **Ad label switched ON** (the names can be slightly different in your app version):
  - **TikTok:** before posting → **More options** → **Disclose post content** → **Branded content**
  - **Instagram:** **Advanced settings** → **Paid partnership label** (if that option isn't there, put **#ad** at the start of the caption)
  - **YouTube Shorts:** in the upload details → **Paid promotion** → tick the box
- ☐ **Only the clip's original audio.** No added trending music.
- ☐ **Submit the link on Whop within minutes of posting.**
- ☐ **Never delete the post, make it private, or edit the caption after submitting.**
- ☐ Log it in the tracker.

### ☐ Step 10 — Sunday review (every Sunday, 30 min) · Dates: ____ / ____ / ____
1. Fill in views and approved/rejected status in the tracker.
2. **Leave** any campaign with a budget under 20% or two or more rejections. Join a new one using Step 6's checks.
3. Look at your 3 best clips. Which hook did they use, and what topic? **Make more of that next week.**

# PHASE C — Your first paying client (from Fri Nov 27, at the same time as Phase B)

This is the path most likely to put your first $20 in your hand.

### ☐ Step 11 — Make your portfolio (Saturday, 1 hour) · Date done: ____
1. Make a Google Drive folder called **"[Your name] — Clip Samples"** and put your **5 best clips** in it. Campaign clips are fine for this.
2. Set the folder's link to "Anyone with the link can view" and copy it.

**Done when:** you have a portfolio link you can paste anywhere.

### ☐ Step 12 — Build a list of 30 prospects (Saturday or Sunday, 1.5 hours) · Date done: ____
Where to look:
- **Instagram:** search "Amman" or "عمان" with your client type (for example "gym Amman", "dentist Amman", "real estate Amman"). Also check **Google Maps** businesses that link to an Instagram account.
- **YouTube and TikTok:** Arabic or Jordanian **podcasts** and creators with **1k–50k followers**.

**A good prospect:**
- ✅ already posts long videos, Lives or talking videos
- ✅ posts few or poor-quality short clips
- ✅ posted in the last 2 weeks

Put them in a new **"Prospects"** tab in your tracker: name · link · type · contacted? · reply · result.

**Done when:** 30 names are in the list.

### ☐ Step 13 — Make free samples for your top 5 (Sunday, 2 hours) · Date done: ____
For your **top 5 prospects**, take **their own** video (a recent Live, podcast or talking video) and make **2 short clips each** with captions.
**Don't post them anywhere.** You'll send them privately.

**Done when:** you have 10 sample clips for 5 prospects.

### ☐ Step 14 — Send your messages (start Monday Nov 30: 10 a day Mon–Thu, 20 a day Fri–Sun) · Date started: ____
Send by Instagram DM, WhatsApp (if the number is public), or email. **Send to the 5 with samples first**, then work through the rest of the list.

**Arabic (for Jordanian or Arab businesses):**
> مرحبا [الاسم] 👋
> أنا [اسمك] من عمّان، بعمل مقاطع قصيرة (ريلز وتيك توك) من الفيديوهات الطويلة.
> شفت فيديو [اسم الفيديو/اللايف] وعملت منه مقطعين جاهزين للنشر — مرفقين هون 👇
> إذا عجبوكم، بقدر أعملكم **10 مقاطع بالشهر بسعر تجريبي 15 دينار** بس للشهر الأول. وإذا ما عجبتكم الشغلة، ما في أي التزام 🙏

**English (for English-speaking creators):**
> Hi [Name] — I'm [your name], a short-form editor. I turned your recent [video/Live] into 2 ready-to-post Reels/TikToks (attached).
> If you like them, I can do **10 clips this month for a $20 intro price**, first month only. No commitment if they're not for you.

For prospects without samples, send the same message but include your **portfolio link** instead of their clips.
**Follow up once after 3 days** if they don't answer: "Just checking you saw these 🙂".

**Done when:** you've sent 50 messages (about one week).

### ☐ Step 15 — Close the deal and get paid **before** you work · Date done: ____
When someone says yes:
1. Confirm in one message: "10 clips, delivered within 14 days, 1 round of changes, 15 JOD, paid via CliQ before I start."
2. Send them your **CliQ alias** (local clients) or your international payment details (foreign clients).
3. **Start the work only after the money arrives.** Log it in the tracker's **Money** tab.

🎉 **When that payment arrives, you've crossed the finish line: your first money from clipping.**

### ☐ Step 16 — If nobody says yes after 50 messages, change one thing and repeat · Date: ____
Don't stop. Change **one** thing at a time, send 30 more messages, and check again:
1. **Make the offer smaller:** "5 clips for 7 JOD" or "I'll clip your last Live for 5 JOD."
2. **Change the type of business:** gyms not replying? Try real estate agents, clinics or podcasters.
3. **Change who gets samples:** send free samples to 10 more prospects. Samples are what close deals.
4. **Ask friends and family:** "Do you know any business or creator who posts videos?" Warm introductions convert much better than cold messages.

### ☐ Step 17 — Deliver great work, then ask for 2 things · Date done: ____
1. Deliver on time or early.
2. Ask: **(a)** "Can I share these in my portfolio?" and **(b)** "Do you know anyone else who'd want this?"
3. Offer month 2 at the normal price: **25–50 JOD for 10–15 clips**.

# PHASE D — Extra income source (Weekend of Dec 4–6, 1 hour)

### ☐ Step 18 — Create a Fiverr gig (optional, runs in the background) · Date done: ____
1. Open a **Fiverr** seller account. Connect payouts (Payoneer or bank) and check that it works from Jordan.
2. Create one gig: **"I will turn your podcast or long video into 5 viral Reels/TikToks with captions"** (Arabic and English).
3. Use your 5 portfolio clips as the gig's samples. Price: **$10 for 3 clips, $20 for 6, $35 for 12.**

New gigs often take weeks to get their first order, so treat this as a bonus. Don't rely on it before December 31.

### ☐ Step 19 — Do a test withdrawal on Whop as soon as your balance allows (around Dec 14) · Date done: ____

### ☐ Step 20 — Last campaign posting day: Dec 18 · Date done: ____
Clips posted after this probably won't pay out until January. **Keep posting anyway.** That's January's money. Just don't count it toward December.

- **Path A (campaigns):** $0–$150. A clip that takes off could pay more, but that can't be planned.
