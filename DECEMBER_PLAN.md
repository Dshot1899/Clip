# Get Paid From Clipping by December 31 — Start-Here Plan

_Written Nov 24, 2026. Platform details come from third-party guides that sometimes disagree with each other. **Always read each platform's and campaign's own rules page before you post.**_

---

## 1. Platforms where you get paid with zero followers

These pay you **per 1,000 views on clips you post from your own accounts**. Your follower count doesn't matter; only your views do.

| Platform | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Whop Content Rewards** (whop.com → Content Rewards / "Clipping") | The biggest marketplace. Free to join, no application, no follower minimum. Most rates are $0.10–$3 per 1,000 views, typically about $1. **Start here.** |
| **Vyro** | Some campaigns are listed at up to about $3 per 1,000. Payout minimum is about $10. Individual campaigns *can* require followers. |
| **Clipping.net** | No follower minimum, but each campaign has a **view threshold** (10k–500k) that a clip has to hit before it pays at all. Not ideal for a beginner. |
| **Vues, Ssemble Clip Rewards, Clipz.global, Klippify, Promotions.Market** | Smaller boards. Use them to find more campaigns once you have a routine on Whop. |

"No follower minimum" almost never means "no minimum." Platforms replace it with **per-clip view minimums, review queues and caps**. Your first clips on a brand-new account will often get too few views to qualify. That's normal. Volume and consistency fix it.

### How a campaign works
1. Create a Whop account, open Content Rewards, and connect and verify your TikTok, Instagram and YouTube accounts.
2. Open a campaign. **Read the whole brief**: source videos, length, required tags and mentions, banned content, allowed platforms, minimum views, maximum per clip, and remaining budget.
3. Download the **source the campaign provides**, edit a clip, and post it from your own account.
4. **Submit the post link right away.** Some guides say you have **30 minutes** after posting, so treat it as an instant step.
5. Views are counted over an earning window (sources say 7 days, some say 30), followed by a short hold of about 3 days. Then you withdraw. Withdrawals start at around $1, and the fee depends on the payout method.

### ⚠ Timing for a December 31 payout
Views take up to a week to count, plus a hold, plus withdrawal time. **Clips you post after about December 18 probably won't be paid out by December 31.** Your real posting window is **now until December 18**. Start this week.

## 2. Do you need more than your Galaxy S25 Ultra?

**No. You can start making money with only the phone.**

| Task | Phone only (S25 Ultra) | Also needs a computer or cloud |
|---|---|---|
| Post, submit links, manage accounts | ✅ Best done on the phone. Posting from a real phone looks natural to the platforms | — |
| Edit clips by hand | ✅ CapCut (auto-captions, 9:16 reframe, keyframes) or the Samsung editor | — |
| Automated pipeline (transcribe, find the hooks, auto-reframe, karaoke subtitles, metadata) | ❌ | ✅ A laptop or PC (16 GB RAM is fine; a GPU helps but isn't required) **or** a Claude Code cloud session like this one, sending finished clips to your phone through Google Drive |

**Recommendation:**
- **Week 1:** phone only. Make 3–5 clips a day in CapCut to learn what gets approved.
- **Week 2:** add the pipeline (cloud session or PC) to go from 5 clips a day to 20–40.
- Nothing has to be bought. If you have no PC, the cloud option works, but **test it with one real campaign source first**. Some sites (especially YouTube) block downloads from cloud servers. Campaign sources on Google Drive usually work.

Optional, cheap upgrades: a **wired or Bluetooth keyboard** for writing captions faster, and **extra cloud storage** (Google One 100 GB) for moving clips.

## 3. The plan, with the questions you need to settle built into each step

### Days 1–2 (Nov 24–25): settle the basics before you post anything
- [ ] **Are you 18 or older, and can you receive payouts in your country?** Whop pays out through Stripe and similar processors, which require legal age and identity verification. Check that your country and payout method (bank, PayPal and so on) are supported **before** doing any work.
- [ ] **Which country is your audience in?** Many campaigns only count views from specific countries, or pay more for US/UK/Canada/Australia views. Write down which geography each campaign accepts. If your accounts mostly reach a region the brief doesn't count, those views may not pay.
- [ ] **Do campaign clips need an ad label?** Campaign clips are **paid promotion**. Many briefs require "Paid partnership," #ad or a similar disclosure, and the law in many countries requires it anyway. Find each brief's rule and follow it every time.
- [ ] **How will you handle taxes?** This is self-employment income. Keep a simple spreadsheet of every payout from the first dollar, and set aside part of each payout. Find out what your country requires (in the US, for example, platforms may issue tax forms above certain amounts).
- [ ] **How many hours a day can you actually give this?** Under 2 hours: campaigns only, 3–5 clips a day. 4+ hours: campaigns plus client outreach.

### Days 2–5 (Nov 25–28): set up the accounts
- [ ] **How many accounts?** Start with **one account per platform** (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts), all in **one niche**. Don't mass-create accounts. Platforms link accounts made on the same phone, and bans spread.
- [ ] **Warm-up:** spend 15–20 minutes a day on each new account browsing your niche like a real viewer (watch, like, follow) for 2–3 days before your first post.
- [ ] **Profile:** a clear niche name, a profile photo, and a bio along the lines of "Best moments from [niche]". Use the same identity on all three platforms.
- [ ] **Avoid scams:** never pay to join a "clipping Discord," never buy a "clipping course," and prefer campaigns whose budget is **funded up front** on the platform. Whop shows the remaining budget.

### Days 3–18 (Nov 26 – Dec 18): the money window
- [ ] Join **3–5 campaigns**. Pick:
  - Rate ≥ $1 per 1,000
  - **More than 50% of the budget left**
  - A low per-clip minimum and a high cap
  - Long podcasts or streams as the source
  - A deadline after Dec 18
- [ ] Post **3–5 clips a day per platform**. Submit each link within minutes of posting.
- [ ] **Rules that protect your payout:**
  - Never delete a post, make it private, or edit its caption after submitting.
  - Use **only the clip's original audio**. No trending copyrighted music, which leads to YouTube claims and muted TikToks.
  - Never post the identical file on two accounts.
  - Never buy views.
- [ ] **Log every clip** in a tracker: campaign, link, date, hook style used, views at 24 hours, approved or rejected, and the rejection reason.
- [ ] **Every 3 days:** drop campaigns with low budgets or high rejection rates, and make more of the clip types that got approved views.
- [ ] Use the hooks from our earlier list (redacted word, silent open, mid-sentence entry, exact numbers). Give each account a different hook on the same moment, then compare the results.

### Day 7 onward (Dec 1+): the faster, reliable money
- [ ] **Pitch creators directly.** Message 20 a day: podcasters and streamers with 10k–200k subscribers who don't post clips. Include 3 free sample clips **sent privately, not posted**.
- [ ] **Charge up front:** send an invoice for the first month (or at least 50%) **before** delivering the clips. One client at $300–$800 paid in December is more certain than all your campaign views combined.
- [ ] Agree in writing (a simple message is fine): the number of clips per month, which platforms, whose accounts they go on, how many rounds of changes, and the payment date.

### Dec 18 – 31: collect
- [ ] Keep posting. Late-December clips pay out in January, which builds next month's income.
- [ ] Withdraw campaign earnings as soon as their holds clear. Do a test withdrawal **early** (even $1–$5) so payout-method problems show up before the end of the month.
- [ ] Look over your tracker: which campaigns, hooks and niches paid best. That becomes your January plan.

## 4. Honest expectations for December 31
- **Campaigns only, phone only, starting this week:** realistically **$0–$300**. More is possible if one clip takes off, but don't count on it.
- **Campaigns plus 1 client paid up front:** **$300–$1,000+**.
- The single biggest factor isn't followers or tools: it's **posting every day from now until December 18 without breaking the briefs' rules.**
